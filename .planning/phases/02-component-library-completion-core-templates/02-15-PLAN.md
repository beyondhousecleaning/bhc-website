**A. Visual, at desktop width and at 375px.** Check `/`, `/services/deep-cleaning`, `/about-us`,
`/privacy-policy` and a deliberately broken URL such as `/nope` (which serves the 404).

1. The band order on `/` matches UI-SPEC §4: hero warm, trust bar paper, service cards warm,
   before/after paper, process steps tint, FAQ paper, then ONE navy CTA band directly above the
   footer. No two adjacent bands share a tone.
2. Nothing overlaps, nothing is unstyled, and there is no horizontal scrollbar at 375px.
3. The before/after section shows two clearly labelled BEFORE and AFTER panels with the
   "Real homes, real results" copy — a finished-looking placeholder, not a gap and not a broken
   image.
4. On mobile only, the sticky call bar sits at the bottom with two equal actions and does not cover
   the footer's last row. It is absent at desktop width.
5. The rating badge is legible everywhere it appears and never sits on the navy band.

**B. Keyboard, at desktop width.**

6. Press Tab once from the top of the page. The FIRST thing focused is "Skip to content". Press
   Enter, then Tab again — focus should now be inside the main content, not back at the header.
7. Tab through the header nav. At 375px, the menu toggle opens and closes the nav with Enter or
   Space. At desktop the nav is visible without any toggle, and the Services submenu opens from the
   keyboard.
8. Tab to the FAQ. Each question row is reachable and opens and closes with Enter or Space. The
   first answer is already open on arrival.
9. Tab to the reviews area on `/` — it is expected to be ABSENT in Phase 2 (review data arrives in
   Phase 4). Confirm nothing empty or broken is rendered where it would be.
10. The focus ring is clearly visible on EVERY control, including the buttons on the navy CTA band
    and the primary orange buttons. This is the one thing most likely to be wrong: the ring is
    deliberately white on dark bands and ink on orange fills.

**C. JavaScript disabled.** Turn JavaScript off in the browser and reload `/`. The mobile nav
toggle and the FAQ must still open and close — the whole phase's client-boundary argument rests on
this.
