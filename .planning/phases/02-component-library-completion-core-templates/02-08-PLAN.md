<!-- UI-SPEC §9.4. Every route in this plan follows it. -->

Template: Breadcrumbs -> Hero align="centered" (no image, rating INCLUDED) -> Prose ->
          QuoteFormEntry *or* CTABand

Per-route variations:
  /get-a-quote      Prose explaining what happens next + QuoteFormEntry + ProcessSteps
  /customer-login   one clear outbound action to the existing booking portal, plus a Call
                    fallback via QuoteFormEntry. NO login form.
  /gift-cards       one clear outbound action. NO purchase form.
  others            Prose + CTABand

Breadcrumb trail on every one of these: two crumbs, `Home` -> `/` then the page label with no href.
Plan 02-01's rule means only the last crumb carries aria-current="page".

Prose blocks are rendered with `renderBlocks` from `@/content/blocks.jsx` (plan 02-06). Do not
write a second block-to-element mapping in a route file.

The exact <h1> for each, from UI-SPEC §5 — these are already in plan 02-02's PAGE_EXPECTATIONS
table and must match CHARACTER FOR CHARACTER:
  /about-us        The Team Behind Beyond House Cleaning
  /contact-us      Contact Our Warwickshire Cleaning Team        <- no <h1> on the live site
  /checklist       What's Included in Every Clean
  /get-a-quote     Get a Free Cleaning Quote
  /work-with-us    Cleaning Jobs in Warwickshire & the West Midlands   <- no <h1> today
  /gift-cards      House Cleaning Gift Cards                     <- no <h1> today
  /customer-login  Manage Your Cleaning Bookings                 <- no <h1> today

The rating badge is on utility pages too — Lock 3 says EVERY template, and Phase 4 SC-1 names
utility explicitly. Pass `rating={RATING}` from `@/content/site.js`. Do NOT pass `emitSchema`.

TEL: BUDGET. A fully composed page carries up to FOUR tel: links — Header (desktop), StickyCallBar
(mobile), NAPFooter (the one inside <footer>), and QuoteFormEntry's fallback on pages that render
it. Plan 02-02's delta 2(b) caps a page at FOUR for exactly this reason. `/get-a-quote` and
`/customer-login` both render QuoteFormEntry and therefore both reach four once plan 02-13 composes
the layout. Do not "fix" that by removing the fallback and do not tighten the cap to three.
