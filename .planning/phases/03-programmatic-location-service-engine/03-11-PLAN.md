<objective>
Blocks 2 and 6 of every combo page: the town's own opening passage (~180 words) and its local
coverage passage (~130 words), for the 21 towns batch 1 publishes.

Purpose: these are the two blocks a same-service page in a different town shares **nothing** with,
and they are what make the corpus's all-pairs median 0.000. §10.2's third prohibition is the whole
point of the first one: block 2 is where a town's real character goes — the housing stock, the age
of the estates, the parking — and it is deliberately town-owned rather than a "town type" lookup,
because a type taxonomy is the thing that makes 58 pages read as 6 pages.

Output: `web/content/town-prose.js` — roughly 6,500 authored words across 21 towns.
</objective>

prohibition bans a sentence produced by substituting a town name into an otherwise fixed sentence,

except inside `localities` and the paragraph built from it.
