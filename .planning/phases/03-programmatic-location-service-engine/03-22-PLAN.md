  1. every `<title>` is at most 60 characters, measured after entity decoding;
  2. every combo and hub title contains its town — derived from the ROUTE's town segment, not from
     any content module, by slugifying the title and requiring it to contain that segment;
  3. a title omits the brand suffix **only** where adding it would exceed 60. This is the clause that

     `title + ' | ' + BRAND` for every brand-less title and require it to exceed 60.

Deliberately **not** a magic count of how many titles drop the brand: that count changes with every
batch, and a lock that has to be edited on every data change gets edited carelessly.

**Delta 20** — one looping test over combo routes only. On each, take the `<h1>` text as the keyword
phrase, then count how many headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) in the markup half contain that phrase.

import and still asserts the thing §5 cares about: the phrase belongs in the `<h1>` and nowhere else.

This is the direct fix for the one on-page flaw the Arbor Trail teardown names — *"Duplicate H2s
on-page … repeating the exact keyword"* — and it is why the combo template's `<h2>`s are
*What's included*, *How it works* and *What our customers say* rather than the keyword three times.

Both locks need a non-vacuity assertion: at least one page must be checked, or a matcher that stops
matching reads green forever.
