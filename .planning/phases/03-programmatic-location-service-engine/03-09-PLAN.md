<objective>
The eight-by-eight service-variant grid for **move-out cleaning**, on the 18 legacy towns only.

Purpose: `move-out-cleaning` and `end-of-tenancy-cleaning` are one UK query intent and 36 frozen
pages already compete for it. The **structural** fix is refusing to create 40 more, and it belongs
to the towns data (§13-D, §13-S). The **editorial** half is here, and §13-S is explicit that it is
not a resolution on its own — it is what makes the 36 pages D2 freezes readable apart.

end-of-tenancy deck is written for the tenant with a deposit at stake. Two audiences, two
vocabularies, and the acceptance criteria below assert the separation in both directions.

Output: `web/content/variants/move-out-cleaning.js` — roughly 4,500 authored words.
</objective>

Eight different SUBJECTS. Index 0-7 is the `a` value a town carries:

  0. Handing over a house you have owned — what a buyer's first walk-through actually notices.
  1. The garage, the loft and the shed — the spaces that get forgotten on completion day.

  3. Marks left by furniture that has stood in the same place for years.
  4. Appliances that are staying versus appliances that are going.
  5. The outside edges — porch, back door, bins, and what a buyer expects to find.
  6. An empty house with no heating, and often no power or water, and how that changes the job.
  7. What is worth doing and what is not, when you are the one paying and leaving anyway.

*chain*. **Do not write about deposits, check-out reports, inventory clerks, agents or landlords** —

cannibalisation the taxonomy work exists to fix while delta 23 reported green.

    banned shape in a comment.
  - No `h2` may begin `Move-Out Cleaning in ` (delta 20).
