<objective>
The D14 rename. Two service slugs become canonical UK vocabulary, four records have their `h1`,
`crumb` and `title` nouns corrected, and every coupled module moves in the same change.

Purpose: `services.js` currently renders *Post-Construction Cleaning* as an `<h1>` and *Builders
Clean* everywhere else on the same page, and *Regular House Cleaning* as both the `<h1>` and the
crumb for the domestic concept. As it stands the same concept ships two different nouns depending
on which template you land on — and Phase 3 is about to multiply every template by 58.

This phase is a rename as well as a build, and the rename touches state that fails in **three
different ways**: loudly at module load (`reviews.js`), loudly after a build (the lock literals),
and **silently** (`faqs.js` returns `[]` and `FAQAccordion` renders nothing, so a missed key deletes
the FAQ band with every lock green). Closing that third path is the most valuable thing in this plan.

Output: one concept, one noun, everywhere — and a guard that makes the next rename safe.
</objective>
