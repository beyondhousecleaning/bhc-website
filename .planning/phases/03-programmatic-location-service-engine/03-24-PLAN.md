Neither blocked planning or execution — both shipped a default and both are one data-file change to
reverse. But each amends something PROJECT.md records as decided, and a phase spec should not amend a
Key Decision silently.

**Item 1 — D2 says "zero redirects for the 95 live pages"; the phase ships five.**
The five are the `coventry-south` set, 301'd to `south-coventry`. SC-3 requires the Coventry duplicate
resolved and D2 says zero redirects; both cannot hold literally. SC-3 is the more specific and more
recent instruction, and the alternative is exactly the keyword cannibalisation the requirement exists
to fix. **90 of the 95 are untouched**, and `check-redirects.mjs` asserts precisely that split, so a
91st redirect is a red build rather than a discovery. `south-coventry` survived because *South
Coventry* is the most common rendering in the live titles.

**Item 2 — D14 says "the 4 Americanised service slugs 301"; the phase ships two.**
D14's own eight canonical concepts already contain `post-construction-cleaning` and
`short-term-rental-cleaning`, so only two of today's six slugs are non-canonical:
`standard-home-cleaning` → `domestic-cleaning` and `move-out-cleaning` → `end-of-tenancy-cleaning`.
The audit's "4" counted a different set — its service-only slugs. The phase closed the *taxonomy*
half of D14 in full: one concept, one noun on every template, and a parent service page for every
combo slug that needed one. The two disputed slugs would be `post-construction-cleaning` →
`builders-clean` and `short-term-rental-cleaning` → `holiday-let-cleaning`, and **the cost is lower
than it looks**: their display nouns are already *Builders Clean* and *Holiday Let Cleaning* on
screen, so redirecting them later changes URLs only.
