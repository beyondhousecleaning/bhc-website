<objective>
Block A of the town hub — *the town, and us in it* — about 260 words per town, for the 21 towns
batch 1 publishes.

Purpose: §10.3 is explicit that **the hub is deliberately not a reuse of its combos' opening block.**
A hub whose text is a subset of the six pages beneath it is the worst possible signal for the page
meant to be the canonical town entity. Sharing only the locality paragraph puts a hub against its
own combo at J = 0.086 and against another town's hub at 0.000 — and that arithmetic only holds if
this block is genuinely new writing.

The hub's counted-prose bar is 645 words against a 600 floor. A +45 margin is not a margin, so this
block's 260 words are load-bearing rather than nice to have.

Output: `web/content/hub-town-prose.js` — roughly 5,500 authored words.
</objective>
