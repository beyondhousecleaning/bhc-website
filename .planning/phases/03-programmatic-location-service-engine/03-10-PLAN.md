Author the 35 notes for the five services that ship on all 58 towns — `domestic-cleaning`,
`deep-cleaning`, `end-of-tenancy-cleaning`, `apartment-cleaning`, `post-construction-cleaning` —
across all seven counties: Warwickshire, West Midlands, Worcestershire, Staffordshire, Shropshire,

Each note is one `p` block of **at least 85 words** (target ~90).

What a county note is for: the thing about that county's housing, geography or working pattern that
changes this job there. Shropshire and this service is not Birmingham and this service — one is
scattered villages and long drives, the other is dense terraced and semi-detached stock with parking
that has to be planned. Write what is true.

Two rules with gates behind them:
  - **A note may name its own county. It may NOT name a town.** The note renders on every published
    town in that county, so a named town is false on all the others — the same class of error as
    §10.2's first prohibition.
  - **No postcode district anywhere, in copy or comment.** The counties in this phase are reached by
    six postcode areas and the district form is one careless sentence away from being published.
    Name places, never codes.

No address, no phone, no email, no price. Plain UK English. A literal `&`, never the entity.
