Why this component exists

Not one of the numerals above is in the markup. They come from counter-increment on the list item and content: counter() on its disc, so the numbering is the list's own ordinal. From Phase 3 these steps come from a data file that copywriters edit; a hand-typed 1. survives exactly until the first reorder. The disc is --bhc-action with an --bhc-ink numeral at 5.17:1, and it is 44px so it matches every other touch target on the page.
