What a deep clean actually covers

A deep clean is the one that goes behind and underneath. We work top to bottom in every room, so the dust that comes off the picture rails lands on floors we have not done yet rather than on ones we have.

It takes longer than a regular visit, and it is priced before we start. If your kitchen has not been done properly in a year, say so when you book — it changes how long we allow, not what it costs on the day.

Kitchens

  • Oven interior, racks and door glass
  • Extractor, hob and splashback
  • Cupboard fronts, handles and skirting boards
  • Behind and underneath the appliances we can safely move

Our cleaners are DBS-checked and insured, and we send the same person back wherever the schedule allows. They learn your home instead of starting from scratch.

Cancellations and access

Give us 24 hours and there is nothing to pay. Inside 24 hours we charge half the visit, because the slot cannot be filled that late and the cleaner has already committed their day to it.
