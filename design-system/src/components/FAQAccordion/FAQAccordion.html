Questions we get asked

How long does a deep clean take?

Most three-bedroom homes take five to seven hours with two cleaners. We quote the hours before we start, so the price does not move once we are in the door.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most of our regular customers give us a key or a door code. Every cleaner is DBS-checked and we are fully insured.

Do you bring your own products?

Yes, everything including the vacuum. If you would rather we used yours — a particular floor product, or something for an allergy — tell us and we will.

Why this component exists

There is no JavaScript here at all. The user agent owns the open state, so it works with scripting disabled and the page stays a server component. The two closed answers above are already in this document — a closed <details> still ships its full text, so a crawler or a language model reads every answer whatever the browser does with the disclosure. And there is no <details name>: exclusive-accordion behaviour would collapse the answer the reader is halfway through.

It emits no structured data. See the prompt doc for the decision, the date and the CI grep that keeps it that way.
