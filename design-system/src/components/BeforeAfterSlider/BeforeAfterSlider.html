State A — one pair. Real images, real BEFORE/AFTER text, a caption, and data-bhc-photo-state="live"

Why the pending state exists at all

Every other empty-data component in this package renders nothing. This one never does. A missing photograph must not silently remove a section from a page, and no page may block waiting on photography that does not exist yet — which is the whole of Success Criterion 3. The state is signalled to machines by data-bhc-photo-state and to people by copy that reads finished rather than apologetic.

The link sits in the figcaption, outside the track. The track carries tabindex="0" so its two panes can be scrolled by keyboard, and that is only correct while it holds nothing focusable itself.

The live-site bug this prevents

All 199 existing before-and-after exports are 1080×1080 composited social posts with the superseded logo burned in at the top and the words BEFORE and AFTER painted over the photograph. The recoverable panel is about 530 × 690px per half — a 265 × 345 slot at retina density. Labels that are pixels cannot be read by a screen reader, cannot be translated, and cannot be resized. Here they are text.
