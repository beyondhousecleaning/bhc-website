Three properties this card is defined by

No nested footer. The attribution is a <p> with a <cite>. A nested footer is legal inside an article, but the built-HTML suite asserts exactly one footer per page and a rail of three cards would put four on the home page.

No clamp. The quote is capped at 320 characters at the data layer, at a word boundary, so what ships is what displays. A CSS clamp with no client JavaScript behind it hides text from sighted readers that screen readers still receive, and 200% zoom widens the gap.

No link. That is what makes ReviewRail's tabindex="0" correct rather than a redundant tab stop.
