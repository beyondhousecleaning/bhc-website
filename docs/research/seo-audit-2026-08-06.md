# SEO audit — beyondhousecleaning.com (live Webflow site)

Audited 2026-08-06. Tier 0 / free sources only — no DataForSEO, no paid keys.

**Method:** all 115 sitemap URLs crawled and parsed (HTML, headings, meta, links, JSON-LD);
22 Arbor Trail combo pages crawled as a calibration control; subresource weight measured
per page; Overpass / Nominatim / web search for citations. Scripts and raw HTML are not
committed — every number below is reproducible from the method notes in §12.

---

## Headline

The live site is **technically clean and its content is genuinely good**. It is losing on
**semantics and architecture**, not on quality. Five things matter:

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The `<h1>` on 101 of 115 pages is an adjective fragment — `"Reliable & Affordable"` — with no service and no town. The keyword line is a `<div>`. | 🔴 Critical |
| 2 | **Zero** structured data on every page. No `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `AggregateRating`, `BreadcrumbList`. | 🔴 Critical |
| 3 | No hub-and-spoke. Every page footer links the **same arbitrary 4** of the 95 location pages; the other 91 get a median of 4 in-copy links. Won't survive 336 pages. | 🔴 Critical |
| 4 | 4.9★ from 175 reviews exists **only inside a 528 KB JS bundle** — absent from HTML, titles, descriptions and schema. Arbor Trail leads every meta description with theirs. | 🟠 High |
| 5 | Site-wide footer displays `+44 7861 936533` but the `tel:` link dials `07441918832`. | 🟠 High |

**Pin resolved 2026-08-06:** the registered office in Leamington Spa (residential; address held at Companies House). Confirms rather than
changes the map-pack picture — Birmingham is 18.8 mi out, Telford 46.2 — so B/DY/TF/WS/WV are
**organic-only**, which is exactly why finding 3 is the critical path. §7.

**And the big one, reversed:** the goals doc names thin/duplicate content as "the single
biggest risk" for scaling to 336 pages. **Measured, it isn't.** BHC's location pages are
**~2× less similar to each other than Arbor Trail's** (median 5-gram Jaccard 0.049 vs
0.100) and carry **2.3× more unique prose** (821 vs 353 words). Not one of 4,465 page
pairs exceeds 0.21 similarity. Content is the strongest asset here — §5.

## 3. Structured data

### 🔴 Critical — zero JSON-LD, site-wide

```
pages with <script type="application/ld+json">:  0 / 115
schema @types found:                             NONE
Microdata / RDFa:                                none
```

Arbor Trail also has zero (confirmed: 0 of 22 pages crawled). The goals doc calls this "our
opening" — it still is, and it is entirely unexploited. Nothing is malformed; nothing exists.

Missing, in priority order:

| Schema | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `LocalBusiness` (or `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`) + `areaServed` | every page | The core local entity. **Unblocked** — §7 settles it: `areaServed`, **no `streetAddress`** |
| `AggregateRating` | homepage / about | 4.9 from 175 — **but see the caveat below** |
| `Service` + `areaServed` | 6 service pages, 95 combo pages | Ties service to town machine-readably |
| `BreadcrumbList` | all combo pages | Currently **0 pages have breadcrumbs of any kind** |
| `Organization` + `sameAs` | homepage | Links the entity to Facebook / Instagram / GBP |
| `WebSite` | homepage | Sitelinks eligibility |

⚠️ **`AggregateRating` caveat.** Google ignores self-serving review markup — a business
marking up its own aggregate rating is not eligible for review rich results, and
Trustmary-sourced (third-party widget) reviews are equally ineligible. Add
`AggregateRating` for the *entity graph and AI-citation* value, not expecting stars in the
SERP. The real win is putting the rating in **HTML, titles and descriptions** (§7).

⚠️ **Do not add `FAQPage` expecting rich results.** Google retired FAQ rich results for all
sites on 2026-05-07. Keep FAQ content for users; don't build the rebuild around FAQ markup.

## 4. Internal linking

### 🔴 Critical — no hub-and-spoke; discovery won't scale

Technically **there are zero orphans** — all 115 pages have ≥1 internal inbound link. But
the distribution is the problem:

| Inbound internal links | Sitemap pages |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2–4 | 61 |
| 5–19 | 32 |
| 20+ | 22 |

For the 95 location pages: **min 2, median 4, max 114.**

The max-114 pages are the tell. Exactly **four** combo pages sit in the global footer, on all
115 pages:

```
/location/warwickshire/daventry/apartment-cleaning
/location/warwickshire/kenilworth/deep-cleaning
/location/warwickshire/leamington-spa/deep-cleaning
/location/warwickshire/southam/apartment-cleaning
```

There is no logic to that selection — two apartment-cleaning, two deep-cleaning, four
unrelated towns. Those four get 114 inbound links each. The remaining **91 get a median of
4**, all from prose links inside sibling location pages ("*We also provide the same trusted
service for clients in Kenilworth*").

At 95 pages this limps along. At **336 pages** it fails outright: 4 hardcoded footer links
cannot distribute authority across 336 destinations, and prose cross-links between siblings
form a flat mesh with no hierarchy for Google to read.

Confirmed. Two patterns coexist:

## 5. Thin / duplicate content — measured, not guessed

**This is the section that changes the plan.** The prevailing assumption is that
duplication across the 95 location pages is the biggest risk to scaling. Measured against a
control, it is the site's strongest asset.

### Method

Page text extracted, `<script>`/`<style>`/`<nav>` removed, then sentences appearing on >30%
of pages (chrome) stripped. Two metrics on the remaining body text, over all 4,465 page
pairs:

- **5-gram shingle Jaccard** — near-duplicate detection; approximates what Google's
  simhash-style dedup sees. Above ~0.5 = substantially the same page.
- **TF-IDF cosine** — topical overlap.

Arbor Trail's combo pages (22, same three metrics, same script) as the control.

### Result — all 95 location pages

| Metric | Median | Mean | p90 | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5-gram Jaccard (body) | **0.049** | 0.054 | 0.064 | **0.208** |
| TF-IDF cosine (body) | 0.120 | 0.131 | 0.196 | 0.601 |
| 5-gram Jaccard (incl. chrome) | 0.085 | 0.090 | 0.104 | 0.246 |

**Pairs above 0.90 Jaccard: 0. Above 0.75: 0. Above 0.50: 0.**
The single most-similar pair in the entire corpus is
`kenilworth/move-out-cleaning` ↔ `warwick/move-out-cleaning` at **0.208**.

### Within a service, across towns — the cannibalisation cohort

| Service | n | Jaccard median | p90 | Max | Median unique words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `apartment-cleaning` | 19 | 0.057 | 0.070 | 0.122 | 813 |
| `deep-cleaning` | 19 | 0.054 | 0.075 | 0.137 | 820 |
| `domestic-cleaning` | 19 | 0.060 | 0.075 | 0.151 | 781 |
| `end-of-tenancy-cleaning` | 19 | 0.054 | 0.069 | 0.154 | 869 |
| `move-out-cleaning` | 19 | 0.046 | 0.064 | 0.208 | 886 |

**Town-masked control.** Replacing every town name with a single token barely moves the
numbers (e.g. deep-cleaning 0.054 → 0.049). If these were mad-libs pages, masking the town
would make them collapse into near-identity. They don't. The differentiation is in the
prose, not the variable.

The `move-out-cleaning` p90 of 0.064 against a max of 0.208 shows the outlier is a genuine
outlier, not the shape of the distribution.

### Calibration against Arbor Trail

| | BHC (95 pages) | Arbor Trail (22 pages) |
|---|---|---|
| Median 5-gram Jaccard, body | **0.049** | **0.100** |
| p90 | 0.064 | 0.114 |
| Max | 0.208 | 0.156 |
| Median unique (non-boilerplate) words/page | **821** | **353** |
| Boilerplate sentences (>30% of pages) | **2** | **29** |

BHC's pages are **~2× less similar to each other** and carry **2.3× more unique prose**.
Arbor Trail carries 29 repeated boilerplate sentences per page; BHC carries 2.

### Against the plugin's thin-content gates

`claude-seo`'s programmatic gates: ⚠️ warn below 40% unique content, 🛑 hard-stop below 30%.

| | Min | p10 | Median | Below 40% | Below 30% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BHC, all 95 | 53.8% | 58.0% | **66.5%** | **0** | **0** |
| BHC, 22-page subsets (n=12 samples, matched to Arbor) | — | — | **81.1%** (range 78.2–83.7) | 0 | 0 |
| Arbor Trail, 22 | 63.7% | — | 75.2% | 0 | 0 |

Reported honestly: on the raw 95-page figure Arbor's 75.2% *looks* better than BHC's 66.5%,
but that is a sample-size artefact — with only 22 pages there are far fewer pages to collide
with. On a like-for-like 22-page sample BHC scores **81.1%**, and the sample-size-independent
pairwise Jaccard (0.049 vs 0.100) separates them in the same direction. Both metrics agree.

### Eyeball confirmation

Three same-service pages, opening paragraph:

> **Warwick** — *"Our deep cleaning services in Warwick deliver full-scale home hygiene for residents, landlords, and property managers seeking spotless, sanitised homes. We handle everything from skirting boards and tile grout to ovens and appliances…"*
>
> **Rugby** — *"Looking for reliable deep cleaners in Rugby? Beyond House Cleaning provides complete home deep cleaning services that refresh, sanitise, and restore every corner. Our trained professionals work efficiently using proven methods and eco-friendly materials…"*
>
> **Banbury** — *"If you're searching for reliable deep cleaners in Banbury, Beyond House Cleaning is your trusted local choice. Our team combines professionalism, experience, and high standards to make every home shine…"*

Different structure, different sentences, different angle. Not variable substitution.

### Verdict on scaling to 336

**The content model is safe to scale.** It passes the swap test (masking the town doesn't
collapse similarity), it beats the reference competitor on every duplication metric, and no
page pair is anywhere near a near-duplicate threshold.

Two conditions, both from `claude-seo`'s enforcement guidance:

1. **Roll out in batches of 50–100**, monitoring indexation for 2–4 weeks between batches.
   Never publish 240 new pages in one go.
2. **Hold the ~800-unique-word bar.** The measured safety margin comes from depth. If the
   new towns get 300-word pages, this analysis no longer applies to them.

The genuine risk in the 336 plan is **not** duplication. It is §4 — there is no architecture
to link 336 pages together.

#### Address handling — decided 2026-08-06 (Sam): hide it everywhere

CV32 6EQ is a **residential** address, so:

- **GBP** — ✅ **already correct.** Confirmed by Sam 2026-08-06: the profile is set as a
  **service-area business with the address suppressed**, which is what Google requires where
  customers are not served at the location. Nothing to do here — leave it alone.
- **Site footer** — a service-area statement (*"Serving Warwickshire, Coventry and the West
  Midlands"*) plus **one** phone number. **No street address.**
- **Schema** — `LocalBusiness` with `areaServed` and **no `streetAddress`**. Do not put
  CV32 6EQ into JSON-LD: schema ships in the HTML of every page, so publishing it there is
  publishing it, on ~336 pages.

Cost of this choice: a small NAP-matching signal, since Google can't cross-reference a
visible street address between site and GBP. That is the right trade — and it is what most
UK domestic-cleaning SABs do. It **unblocks fixes a6 and b7**, which were previously waiting
on this decision.

## 12. Reproducing this audit

All figures come from a crawl of the 115 sitemap URLs on 2026-08-06 plus 22 Arbor Trail
combo pages as a control. The measurements, in order:

1. Fetch all 115 URLs from `docs/research/current-site-sitemap-2026-08-06.xml`; record
   status, redirect chain, byte size, response headers.
2. Parse each page for title, meta description, canonical, `og:*`, `h1`–`h4`, JSON-LD
   blocks, images + `alt`, and every internal `href`.
3. Similarity: strip `<script>`/`<style>`/`<nav>`, split into sentences >25 chars, drop
   sentences appearing on >30% of pages, then compute 5-gram shingle Jaccard and TF-IDF
   cosine over all pairs — plus a town-masked variant that replaces town names with a token.
4. Repeat step 3 verbatim on the Arbor Trail control set.
5. Weight: fetch every subresource per page with a mobile UA and gzip/br, bucket by
   content-type.
6. Local: `tel:` href vs displayed text across all pages; Overpass and Nominatim around
   CV32; web search for directory citations.

Re-running it after the rebuild gives a like-for-like before/after.
