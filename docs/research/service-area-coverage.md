**Key insight:** these five postcode areas reach far beyond their namesake cities. They include Solihull, Sutton Coldfield, Tamworth, Redditch, Bromsgrove, Halesowen, Stourbridge, Kidderminster, Lichfield, Cannock, Rugeley, Bridgnorth, Market Drayton and Much Wenlock — places residents would never describe as "Birmingham" or "Dudley". Location pages must therefore target **post towns and named suburbs**, never postcode districts.
